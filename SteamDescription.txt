[img]https://URL/food.svg[/img]

[b]Food Prediction[/b] — Shows how many days your food will last based on colonist count and hunger rates.

[list]
[*]Count ALL edible items on stockpiles
[*]Or switch to "Prepared food only" mode — counts meals, not raw ingredients
[*]Respects colony food restrictions (diet policies)
[/list]

[img]https://URL/health.svg[/img]

[b]Medicine Forecast[/b] — Tracks available treatments vs untreated injuries.

[list]
[*]Lists every wounded pawn with injury count
[*]Shows time until death from blood loss (hours, decimal hours, seconds — matches the in-game health tab)
[/list]

[img]https://URL/animals.svg[/img]

[b]Starving Animals[/b] — Detects malnourished or starving animals before it's too late.

[img]https://URL/doctor.svg[/img]

[b]Doctor Status[/b] — Warns if ALL your doctors are downed or incapacitated.

[img]https://URL/repair.svg[/img]

[b]Repair Resources[/b] — Lists damaged buildings that can't be repaired due to missing materials.

[img]https://URL/immunity.svg[/img]

[b]Immunity Tracker[/b] — Alerts when infection severity outpaces immunity progress.

[img]https://URL/config.svg[/img]

[b]Configuration[/b] — All options in Menu → Options → Mod Settings → Colony Analytics.

[list]
[*]Toggle each alert on/off independently
[*]Set custom thresholds (e.g., warn when food < 3 days)
[*]Switch between nutrition-based and meal-based food counting
[*]Configure minimum medicine treatment count
[*]One-time welcome dialog with quick-access to settings
[/list]
