Shows how many days your food will last, factoring in colonist count and hunger rates.

[list]
[*]Counts ALL edible items on stockpiles
[*]Or switch to "Prepared food only" mode — counts meals, not raw ingredients
[*]Respects colony food restrictions (diet policies)
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DiscoD3979/ColonyAnalytics/main/health_en.png[/img]

Tracks available treatments against untreated injuries.

[list]
[*]Lists every wounded pawn with injury count
[*]Shows time until death from blood loss (hours, decimal hours, seconds — matches the in-game health tab)
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DiscoD3979/ColonyAnalytics/main/animals_en.png[/img]

Detects malnourished or starving animals before it's too late.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DiscoD3979/ColonyAnalytics/main/doctor_en.png[/img]

Warns if ALL your doctors are downed or incapacitated.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DiscoD3979/ColonyAnalytics/main/repair_en.png[/img]

Lists damaged buildings that can't be fixed due to missing materials.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DiscoD3979/ColonyAnalytics/main/immunity_en.png[/img]

Alerts when infection severity outpaces immunity progress.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DiscoD3979/ColonyAnalytics/main/config_en.png[/img]

All options in Menu → Options → Mod Settings → Colony Analytics.

[list]
[*]Toggle each alert on/off independently
[*]Set custom thresholds (e.g., warn when food < 3 days)
[*]Switch between nutrition-based and meal-based food counting
[*]Configure minimum medicine treatment count
[*]One-time welcome dialog with quick access to settings
[/list]
